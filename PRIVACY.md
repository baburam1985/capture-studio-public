# Capture Studio — Screenshots & PDF

## Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026. Last updated September 30, 2026.

Capture Studio is a local screenshot, annotation, document export and screen recording extension.

## What the extension handles

When you request a capture, the extension processes the visible content of the selected page or screen, including any personal information it contains. It stores screenshots, optional recordings and audio, page titles and source URLs, annotations, recognized text, tags, folders and settings in your local Chrome profile. Editable projects retain source pixels even when covered with annotations.

## No uploads, accounts or analytics

The extension does not transmit captures, page contents, recognized text, audio, settings or usage analytics to the publisher or a third party. OCR code, language models and fonts are bundled and run on your device. There are no extension accounts, advertising, tracking or cloud backups.

## User-directed actions

Batch capture opens the web addresses you supply. Those websites receive the normal browser requests, cookies and network information involved in visiting them. This is distinct from uploading screenshots. Downloads are saved through Chrome, clipboard copies are available to apps you paste into, and files you choose to share are controlled by you.

## Permissions

Temporary active-tab access and scripting enable captures you start. Local storage holds preferences and capture status; the local database holds your library. Downloads save files you request. Context menus and keyboard commands provide capture shortcuts. Alarms apply your chosen retention period. Batch capture requests optional website access. Clipboard write access is requested when needed. Screen and microphone capture use the browser's permission prompts.

## Retention and deletion

Captures remain until you delete them or your configured retention period expires. Favorites are excluded from automatic cleanup. Deleting a capture deletes its locally stored project and recording. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage; files already downloaded remain on disk. Browser profile backups or operating-system backups are outside the extension's control.

## Safety of exported files

Image and PDF exports flatten annotations. Searchable PDFs recognize text from the final flattened image. Editable project exports may contain the underlying original screenshot; share flattened exports when concealing information. Automated sensitive-information suggestions can miss content and must be reviewed. Review filenames and PDF titles, which may include the captured page title. Optional source details or the Include page URL option can include the source URL in exports.

## Limited use

Capture Studio uses captured content and source-page information only to provide its screenshot, editing, organization, OCR, export and recording features. This use complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The publisher does not receive or review your captures through the extension. The extension does not sell data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness or unrelated purposes.
